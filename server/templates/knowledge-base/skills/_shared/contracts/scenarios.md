# scenarios Contract

Use for executable business scenarios, agentic automations, monitoring flows, and operational playbooks.

Required when supported:
- Scenario goal and business context.
- Trigger: schedule, user request, upload, email, event, or upstream state.
- Scan scope: records, files, messages, tables, systems, or time window.
- Required inputs and prerequisites.
- Detection or eligibility conditions.
- Ordered execution steps.
- Branches, short-circuit logic, priority order, fallback, and skip behavior.
- Required knowledge dependencies: data tables, mapping rules, templates, policies, interfaces, or glossary terms.
- Actions: query, calculate, write, notify, route, call an interface, or ask user for confirmation.
- Output format and user-facing result.
- Closure criteria, retry/reminder cadence, no-match behavior, and human review points.

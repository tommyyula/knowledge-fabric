# policies Contract

Use for reusable decision policies: routing, escalation, approval, ownership, priority, SLA, threshold, match, or exception policies.

Required when supported:
- Policy purpose and when to apply it.
- Input signals and required fields.
- Decision table, match keys, condition order, or priority.
- Owner/recipient/route mapping and fallback.
- Thresholds, SLA rules, formulas, or business-day rules.
- Output action, notification, approval, escalation, or block.
- No-match behavior, exceptions, and conflict handling.
- Related scenarios, templates, and data tables.

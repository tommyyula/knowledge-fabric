# business_capabilities Contract

Required when supported:
- Capability purpose and business value.
- User intents, triggers, or situations that invoke the capability.
- Actors, systems, modules, and data dependencies involved.
- Inputs, prerequisites, and permissions.
- Core behavior and major sub-capabilities.
- Outputs, state changes, side effects, or user-visible results.
- Constraints, failure states, exclusions, and known risks.
- Related flows, interfaces, business objects, and rules.

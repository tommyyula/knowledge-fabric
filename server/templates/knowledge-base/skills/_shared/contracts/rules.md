# rules Contract

Use for reusable business or system constraints that are not better represented as a full flow or policy.

Required when supported:
- Rule purpose and applicability.
- Input signals or fields.
- Decision logic, thresholds, formulas, or condition table.
- Priority, fallback, and conflict behavior.
- Output decision, action, state, or validation result.
- Exceptions and negative constraints.
- Related objects, flows, policies, or interfaces.

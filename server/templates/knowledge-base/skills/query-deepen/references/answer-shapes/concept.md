# Concept Answer Shape

Use when the subject is a concept, object, module, capability, or business entity.

Cover:
- what it is and why it exists
- where it sits in the business or system stack
- key components, fields, roles, or dimensions
- how it typically operates or changes over time
- related objects, rules, interfaces, or workflows
- boundaries, caveats, and knowledge gaps

If the user asks about its "flow", include only the lifecycle or operation details that clarify the concept.
